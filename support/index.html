Support

Andogam Descent · iPhone

Something broken, confusing or missing? Email andogamdescent@gmail.com and say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Purchases

  • What is free? The first two worlds, replayable for ever. One one-time purchase unlocks the rest of the descent and Earth. There are no subscriptions and no ads.
  • I bought it and it is locked. On the purchase screen, tap Already bought it? Restore purchase. You need to be signed in to the same Apple ID that bought it.
  • I changed phones. Restore the purchase as above. Your game progress is stored on the device and does not travel with the purchase.
  • Refunds. Apple handles these. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

Saves and progress

  • Everything is stored on your phone. There is no account and no cloud copy, so deleting the app deletes your progress.
  • Pause → Reset Everything clears your unlocks on purpose. It asks twice.

Co-op

  • Co-op is for two phones in the same room. Both need the same version of the game, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on, and Local Network allowed when iOS asks.
  • If the other phone never appears, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and make sure Andogam Descent is switched on.

Controllers

  • Any iOS-compatible Bluetooth controller works. Remap buttons from Pause → Settings → Controller.

Other

  • Left-handed layout and auto-fire are in Pause → Settings.
  • The game needs iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone only.
  • Our privacy policy.
