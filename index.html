Privacy Policy

Andogam Descent · last updated 12 October 2026

Andogam Descent does not collect, transmit, or share any data about you. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account. The app has no network code in it at all.

What is collected

Nothing. No personal information, no usage data, no identifiers, no diagnostics, no crash reporting.

Network access

The app makes no requests to any server of ours or of anyone else's. It contains no third-party SDKs and no external dependencies. The game runs entirely from files inside the app bundle and is fully playable offline and in Airplane Mode. Two things touch a network, and neither sends anything to us:

  • The optional purchase. Unlocking the full game is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple's App Store. Payment details go to Apple and never reach us, and we never see them.
  • Optional two-player co-op. If you start a co-op game, the two phones talk directly to each other over the local network using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity. They exchange game state only, nothing passes through any server, and nothing is kept once the session ends. iOS asks your permission first (Local Network).

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress so you can continue where you left off. This includes things like research points, unlocked suits and upgrades, difficulty progress, recovered datapads, audio volume settings, and any run in progress.

  • It is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage.
  • It is never uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone.
  • It contains no personal information — only game state.
  • It is permanently deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app is safe for all ages with respect to privacy: because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either. It contains no ads, no chat, no user-generated content, and no links out to the web. Its one in-app purchase is processed by Apple.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no cloud services, no social logins. Purchases are processed by Apple, which is the App Store itself rather than a party we share data with.

Your rights

Since no data ever reaches us, there is no data for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. Deleting the app permanently erases everything it has stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date. Any change that affected data collection would also require a new app release, which is reviewed by Apple.
